## Interpretation notes

- Road-class-only raw recall can be high when reporting concentrates on particular road classes. The headline stratifies within major/mid/minor groups; the three-class-only synthetic test returns about 10% stratified recall.
- Unknown routes are treated as negatives with sample weight 0.2, per design; routes with an eligible observation but no target-cause positive receive weight 1.0.
- T3–T5 use 2 km UTM blocks assigned to five folds by fixed seed; block identifiers and coordinates remain inside the harness.
- This report is baseline evidence only. It does not use 2025+ records and it does not train a production model.
